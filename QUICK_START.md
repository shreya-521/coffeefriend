# 🚀 CoffeeFriend PWA - Quick Start

## 📱 For Your Mobile Users

### **iPhone / iPad:**
1. Open Safari and go to: `[your-app-url]/install.html`
2. Tap the Share button ↗️
3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
4. Tap "Add"
5. App installed! ☕

### **Android:**
1. Open Chrome and go to: `[your-app-url]/install.html`
2. Tap Menu ⋮ (three dots)
3. Tap "Install app"
4. Confirm
5. App installed! ☕
