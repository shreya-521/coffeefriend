Latte

  • Category: Mild coffee
  • Time: 5 min
Ingredients
  • 1 shot espresso
  • 200 ml steamed milk
  • Foamed milk to top
Steps
  1. Pull a single espresso shot into a cup.
  2. Steam milk until silky microfoam forms.
  3. Pour milk into espresso, holding back foam, then finish with a spoon of foam.

Cappuccino

  • Category: Mild coffees
  • Time: 5 min
Ingredients
  • 1 shot espresso
  • 60 ml steamed milk
  • 60 ml milk foam
  • Cocoa or cinnamon (optional)
Steps
  1. Pull an espresso shot.
  2. Steam milk to medium foam.
  3. Pour equal parts espresso, steamed milk, and foam. Dust with spice.

Flat White

  • Category: Mild coffees
  • Time: 5 min
Ingredients
  • 1 double shot espresso
  • 120 ml steamed milk (fine microfoam)
Steps
  1. Pull a double espresso.
  2. Steam milk to a fine, glossy microfoam.
  3. Pour milk to create a thin, velvety layer.

Espresso

  • Category: Very strong coffee
  • Time: 2 min
Ingredients
  • 18 g finely ground coffee
  • Water at 93°C
Steps
  1. Distribute and tamp grounds evenly.
  2. Pull 25–30 seconds for ~36 g out.
  3. Serve immediately.

Ristretto

  • Category: Very strong coffee
  • Time: 2 min
Ingredients
  • 18 g finely ground coffee
  • Water at 93°C
Steps
  1. Prepare as espresso.
  2. Stop extraction earlier at ~20–25 g yield for intense flavor.

Mocha

  • Category: Mild coffee
  • Time: 6 min
Ingredients
  • 1 shot espresso
  • 150 ml steamed milk
  • 20 g chocolate syrup or cocoa
  • Whipped cream (optional)
Steps
  1. Stir chocolate into espresso.
  2. Add steamed milk and mix gently.
  3. Top with whipped cream if desired.

Café au Lait

  • Category: Mild coffee
  • Time: 4 min
Ingredients
  • 1 cup hot brewed coffee
  • 1 cup steamed milk
Steps
  1. Brew filter coffee.
  2. Steam milk until hot and slightly foamy.
  3. Combine equal parts coffee and milk.

Americano

  • Category: Medium coffee
  • Time: 3 min
Ingredients
  • 1 shot espresso
  • 120–180 ml hot water
Steps
  1. Pour hot water into a cup.
  2. Add espresso on top to preserve crema.

Cortado

  • Category: Medium coffee
  • Time: 3 min
Ingredients
  • 1 shot espresso
  • 1 shot steamed milk
Steps
  1. Pull an espresso shot.
  2. Steam a small amount of milk (minimal foam).
  3. Combine 1:1 milk to espresso.

Doppio

  • Category: Very strong coffee
  • Time: 3 min
Ingredients
  • 18–20 g finely ground coffee
  • Water at 93°C
Steps
  1. Pull a double espresso shot (~36–40 g out in ~25–30 s).
  2. Serve in a prewarmed demitasse.

Moka Pot

  • Category: Very strong coffee
  • Time: 6–8 min
Ingredients
  • Medium-fine ground coffee
  • Water (fill bottom chamber to valve)
Steps
  1. Fill base with hot water; add grounds to basket (level, untamped).
  2. Assemble and brew on medium heat until you hear a gentle gurgle.
  3. Remove from heat and serve immediately.

Iced Latte

  • Category: Mild coffee
  • Time: 3 min
Ingredients
  • 1 shot espresso (cooled)
  • 150 ml cold milk
  • Ice cubes
Steps
  1. Fill a glass with ice.
  2. Add cold milk, then pour espresso over.
  3. Stir gently.

Caramel Latte

  • Category: Mild coffee
  • Time: 6 min
Ingredients
  • 1 shot espresso
  • 180 ml steamed milk
  • 2 tsp caramel sauce
Steps
  1. Stir caramel into espresso.
  2. Add steamed milk and mix.
  3. Optional: drizzle more caramel on top.

Long Black

  • Category: Medium coffee
  • Time: 3 min
Ingredients
  • 1 double espresso
  • 120 ml hot water
Steps
  1. Add hot water to a cup.
  2. Pour double espresso over to preserve crema.

Lungo

  • Category: Very strong coffee
  • Time: 3–4 min
Ingredients
  • 18 g finely ground coffee
  • Water at 93°C
Steps
  1. Pull a longer espresso (~50–60 g out).
  2. Expect a more bitter, intense cup.
