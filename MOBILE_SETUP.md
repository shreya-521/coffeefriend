# ☕ CoffeeFriend Mobile App Setup

Your CoffeeFriend app is now a **Progressive Web App (PWA)** — it can be downloaded and used offline on any mobile device!

## 📱 Installation Instructions

### iOS (iPhone/iPad)
1. Open Safari and navigate to your app URL
2. Tap the **Share** button (square with arrow)
3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
4. Enter app name and tap **"Add"**
5. The app now appears on your home screen with offline access

### Android
1. Open Chrome (or your default browser)
2. Navigate to your app URL
3. Tap the **⋮ menu** (three dots)
4. Tap **"Install app"** or **"Add to Home screen"**
5. Confirm the installation
6. The app now appears on your home screen with offline access

## 🚀 Features

✅ **Offline-First** — Browse all content without internet  
✅ **Fast Loading** — App caches on first visit  
✅ **Lightweight** — ~40KB total (images cached locally)  
✅ **Mobile Optimized** — Perfect on any screen size  
✅ **App-Like Feel** — Fullscreen, no browser UI  
✅ **Theme Support** — Light/Dark mode preference saved  

## 📋 What's Included

- **Today's Coffee Mood** — Discover your perfect brew
- **Pairings** — Coffee food combinations
- **Fun Facts** — Learn about beverages offline
- **Theme Toggle** — Light & dark modes (saved locally)

## 💾 Storage & Performance

- **Total Cache Size**: ~2MB (minimal mobile footprint)
- **First Load**: Caches all files for instant offline access
- **Network**: Works with or without connection
- **Updates**: New version cache in background

## 🔧 How It Works

1. **Service Worker** (`sw.js`) — Handles offline caching
2. **Web Manifest** (`manifest.json`) — Defines app metadata & icons
3. **Responsive CSS** — Adapts to any screen size
4. **Local Storage** — Remembers your theme preference

## 🌐 Online/Offline Status

- **Online**: App fetches latest content + caches for offline use
- **Offline**: App serves all content from cache (instant loading)
- **Reconnect**: New content automatically cached in background
